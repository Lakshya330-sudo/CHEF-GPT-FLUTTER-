# ChefGPT: Brand Guidelines
### *Cook Smart, Eat Well.*

---

## 🤖 About the App

**ChefGPT** is an AI-powered Indian recipe assistant designed to reduce food waste and promote smarter cooking. Users can photograph the ingredients in their fridge, and ChefGPT identifies them and suggests healthy, personalised Indian recipes — complete with step-by-step cooking instructions.

**Core Features:**
- 📸 Snap-to-cook ingredient recognition
- 🥗 Dietary filter support (Vegan, Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Gluten-Free, Jain)
- 🍳 Cooking level selection (Easy, Medium, Pro)
- 🛒 Local Bazaar Deals & Pantry tracking
- 📋 Meal Plans, Recipe History & Food Waste stats
- 🧰 Kitchen tool awareness (Induction, Pressure Cooker, Microwave, Blender)

**Tagline:** *Reduce Food Waste with Indian AI Recipes*

## 🎨 Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Spice Red** | `#E63946` | Buttons, Highlights, CTAs |
| **Roti Beige** | `#FFF8E1` | Background, App Surface |
| **Charcoal Ink** | `#1A1A1A` | Primary Text, Icons |
| **Pudina Green** | `#4CAF76` | Success States, Accents, Toggles |

### Usage Notes
- **Spice Red** is the primary action color — used for all primary buttons (LOGIN, GET STARTED, SHOW ME RECIPES, COOK THIS) and interactive highlights.
- **Roti Beige** creates the warm, inviting background that gives the app its homely, Indian kitchen aesthetic.
- **Charcoal Ink** ensures high readability for all body text and iconography.
- **Pudina Green** signals positive states — confirmed ingredients, enabled dietary filters, and success indicators.

## ✍️ Typography

**Primary Typeface:** `Poppins`

Poppins is a geometric sans-serif typeface that balances friendliness with clarity — perfectly matching ChefGPT's approachable yet informative tone.

| Style | Weight | Usage |
|-------|--------|-------|
| **Primary Headings** | Bold / ExtraBold | Screen titles, Feature headers (e.g., *"Cook Smart, Eat Well"*) |
| **Subheadings** | SemiBold | Section labels, Card titles (e.g., *"What's in your fridge?"*) |
| **Body Text** | Regular | Descriptions, Ingredient lists, Recipe steps |
| **UI Labels** | Medium | Buttons, Tags, Captions (e.g., *"30 MIN · EASY · HIGH PROTEIN"*) |

### Typography Rules
- Headings should feel bold and energetic — never thin or delicate.
- Use sentence case for most UI labels; all-caps only for strong CTAs (e.g., `GET INDIAN RECIPES!`).
- Maintain generous line-height for readability in recipe step content.

## 🎭 Brand Personality

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Warm** | Feels like a helpful friend in your kitchen, not a cold tech product |
| **Playful** | Cartoon illustrations, friendly mascot, fun micro-interactions |
| **Smart** | AI-powered, data-driven, but never overwhelming |
| **Cultural** | Deeply rooted in Indian cuisine, ingredients, and cooking traditions |
| **Purposeful** | Driven by a clear mission — reduce food waste, eat healthier |

---

## 📐 UI Design Principles

- **Rounded corners** on all cards, buttons, and containers — softness is key.
- **Illustration-first** — custom cartoon icons for dietary filters, kitchen tools, and mascot moments.
- **High contrast CTAs** — Spice Red buttons on Roti Beige backgrounds create clear, unmissable actions.
- **Warm & airy layouts** — generous whitespace against the beige background keeps the UI breathable.
- **Consistent badge system** — green "Correct?" confirmation badges and red action buttons follow a predictable visual language throughout.
